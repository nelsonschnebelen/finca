The Standard

What the number means.

The most common reason an event quote falls apart is that the figure on the page isn’t the figure on the contract. Here is exactly what ours includes.

The price is the price

$85 or $65 per guest, plus a 20% gratuity and California sales tax. That is the entire bill — there is no third line waiting for you at signing.

No room fee, no space fee

We don’t charge you for the space. The only exception is a complete buyout of the restaurant, which is quoted on its own.

Dietary needs, no upcharge

Vegan, gluten-free, allergies — flag them once and the chef adjusts the menu or sends out additional dishes. There is no fee for accommodating your table.

Abundance is the point

Everything is shared, and it is meant to be more than enough. Your guests should leave having tasted the whole menu — not a portion of it.
